# BGE small Chinese v1.5

- Base model: `BAAI/bge-small-zh-v1.5`
- Browser-compatible ONNX conversion: `Xenova/bge-small-zh-v1.5`
- Source: https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-small-zh-v1.5
- ONNX source: https://huggingface.co/Xenova/bge-small-zh-v1.5
- License declared by the model repository: MIT

This copy is bundled so Level Grind can perform Chinese semantic retrieval
locally in the browser without sending Claims or search queries to an external
embedding API.
